Who We Are

About CBS Wine

A student-run wine society bringing world-class tastings to Copenhagen Business School.

Our Mission

CBS Wine exists to make wine accessible, enjoyable, and educational for students. We believe you don't need a sommelier certification to appreciate great wine — just curiosity and good company.

Founded in 2024, we organize 2–3 tasting events per semester at CBS, each attended by 50–100 students. We partner with professional wine importers and merchants who bring curated selections and share their knowledge. Every tasting is designed to be approachable for beginners while still engaging for those with more experience.

What We Offer

🍷

Expert-Led Tastings

Professional wine importers present 4+ wines per event, sharing stories about regions, grapes, and producers.

💰

Student-Friendly Prices

Tastings from 100 kr — far below what you'd pay at a commercial tasting or wine bar.

🤝

Community & Networking

Meet fellow CBS students who share your interest in wine, food, and culture. Great conversations guaranteed.
