Who We Are

About CBS Wine

A student-run wine society bringing world-class tastings to Copenhagen Business School.

Our Mission

CBS Wine exists to make wine accessible, enjoyable, and educational for students. We believe you don't need a sommelier certification to appreciate great wine — just curiosity and good company.

We organize up to 3 tasting events per semester at CBS, each attended by up to 100 students. We collaborate with professional wine importers and merchants who bring curated selections and share their knowledge. Every tasting is designed to be approachable for beginners while still engaging for those with more experience.

What We Offer

🍷

Expert-Led Tastings

Professional wine importers present 4+ wines per event, sharing stories about regions, grapes, and producers.

💰

Student-Friendly Prices

Tastings from 100 kr — far below what you'd pay at a commercial tasting or wine bar.

🤝

Community & Networking

Meet fellow CBS students who share your interest in wine, food, and culture. Great conversations guaranteed.

Join CBS Wine

We're always happy to meet wine-curious CBS students who want to get involved.

CBS Wine is run by a small group of students who share a love for wine, events, and good company. We plan tastings, reach out to the wine industry, create content, and handle all the logistics that make our events happen. We recruit new members on a rolling basis whenever spots open up — no formal application process, just send us a message.
